My Dearest Tykeisha,

Today the world added another year to your name. But I want you to know something that no calendar can measure: you have always been here. In the quiet places I carried without knowing what they were. In the questions I never let myself ask. In the space that could not be filled, no matter how much life I poured into it.

You spent thirty years searching for someone who should have spent every one of those years searching for you. That is a truth I will carry for the rest of my life. Not as an excuse, not as something to be explained away, but as the most humbling and clarifying fact of my existence. You found me. You did what I failed to do. And in doing so, you showed me who you are.

You said you felt it in every fiber of your being, that somewhere out there was a father who was yours. You were right. You have always been right. And you never stopped until you found the truth.

That courage, that unrelenting, quiet, powerful certainty, that is yours. It did not come from circumstance. It did not come from having all the answers. It came from something deep inside you that the years and the distance and the silence could not touch. I see myself in you in ways that make me proud and humble me at the same time.

You drive like me. People say you look like me. But more than any of that, you never gave up. And neither will I. Not today. Not from this birthday forward.

On the day you were born, something was set into motion that time could not stop. And on this birthday, I am choosing to finally show up. Not because I deserve the chance. But because you deserve the father. And it is never, ever too late to begin.

Thank you for finding me. Thank you for being the kind of person who never stopped believing there was something worth finding. You are the most extraordinary reminder that love does not expire. It simply waits.

With all the love that was always yours, Your Father Daniel Girtley III
